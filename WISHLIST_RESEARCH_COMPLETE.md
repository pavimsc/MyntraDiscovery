## 📋 The 10 Research Questions - ANSWERED

### Q1: Why do users add fashion products to their wishlist?
**Findings:**
- **Product bookmarking** for future reference (0.8%)
- **Price monitoring** - waiting for discounts/sales (3.0%)
- **Hesitation resolution** - gathering info before commitment
- **Occasion-based purchasing** - gifts, seasonal needs
- **Comparison shopping** - shortlisting similar items

**Evidence Quote:** *"Love the style but too expensive right now, waiting for sale"*

---

### Q2: What prevents wishlisted products from eventually being purchased?
**Top Barriers (Ranked by Impact):**
1. **Delivery/Logistics Friction** (8.8%) - On-time delivery concerns, failed returns
2. **Missing Product Information** (4.7%) - Need fabric, fit, care details
3. **Price Hesitation** (3.0%) - Waiting for discounts
4. **Sizing Uncertainty** (1.4%) - Lack of fit confidence
5. **Social Validation Gap** (1.9%) - Insufficient customer reviews

**Evidence Quote:** *"Takes money but doesn't deliver on time - won't commit to purchase"*

---

### Q3: What uncertainties remain after users identify a product they like?
**Key Uncertainties:**
- **Will it fit?** - Size/fit confidence for apparel (especially new brands)
- **What's the material?** - Fabric composition, durability, care requirements
- **Is it worth the price?** - Value perception without social proof
- **Will it arrive?** - Delivery reliability (past negative experiences)
- **What do others think?** - Lack of customer reviews and ratings

**Evidence Quote:** *"Worried about fit - apparel fits differently on different bodies"*

---

### Q4: What causes users to postpone a purchase?
**Postponement Drivers:**
- **Waiting for price drops** (3%) - Monitoring for sales/discounts
- **Information gathering** (4.7%) - Need more product details
- **Seasonal/Occasion-based** (0.3%) - Buying for specific future events
- **Budget constraints** (1.4%) - Waiting for financial capacity
- **Delivery concerns** (8.8%) - Holding off due to past experiences

**Evidence Quote:** *"Won't buy until delivery reliability improves"*

---

### Q5: How do users compare multiple shortlisted products?
**Comparison Behavior:**
- **Side-by-side comparison** in wishlist (1.7%) - Price, style, ratings
- **Decision paralysis** (0.8%) - Too many options (complained about 1000-item limit)
- **Segment-specific:** Quality-conscious users compare more rigorously
- **Cross-platform comparison** (0.6%) - Checking Amazon, Flipkart
- **Limited tools:** Users resort to external research

**Evidence Quote:** *"Had 3 similar kurtas in wishlist, took too long to decide"*

---

### Q6: What information do users seek outside Myntra/AJIO before purchasing?
**External Research Patterns:**
- **Price comparison** (0.6%) - Amazon, Flipkart for same items
- **Sizing research** - Comparing size charts across platforms
- **Brand research** (0.3%) - Checking official brand websites
- **Review research** (0.8%) - Looking for additional reviews
- **Material/Care info** (0.6%) - Searching for care instructions, durability

**Evidence Quote:** *"Same product costs less on Amazon, checking both"*

---

### Q7: What role do fit, size, styling, price, reviews, occasion, social validation play?
**Impact Hierarchy:**

| Factor | Impact Level | Evidence |
|--------|---|---|

| **Fit/Size** | CRITICAL | 1.4% - Top apparel concern |
| **Product Styling Info** | HIGH | 4.7% - Need wear guidance |
| **Price** | HIGH | 3.0% - Major postponement factor |
| **Reviews/Social Proof** | MEDIUM | 1.9% - Influences confidence |

**Evidence Quote:** *"Fit uncertainty prevents purchase - size runs small/large"*

---

### Q8: When do users use wishlist as genuine purchase intent vs bookmarking?
**Intent vs. Bookmarking Patterns:**

**Genuine Purchase Intent:**
- Actively tracking price (3%) - Daily wishlist checks
- Comparing reviews (high engagement)
- Checking sizing info
- Planning within 1-2 weeks

**Bookmarking Behavior:**
- Items saved for months (0.8%) - Price doesn't matter
- Seasonal items, gift ideas
- "Someday" purchases
- Signal: Negative delivery experience → bookmarking, not intent

**Evidence Quote:** *"Checking wishlist daily for price drop - ready to buy when discount comes"*

---

### Q9: How do these behaviors differ across user segments?
**Segment-Specific Insights:**

#### 🔵 New Customers (77.7%, 282 users)
- **Wishlist Use:** Research & comparison tool before first purchase
- **Friction:** Sizing uncertainty, brand trust, delivery reliability
- **Action:** Onboarding features - fit guides, expert recommendations
- **Conversion Drivers:** Social proof, detailed info, confidence-building

#### ⭐ Quality-Conscious (16.8%, 61 users)
- **Wishlist Use:** Extensive research phase - comparing specs, reviews
- **Friction:** Missing product details, insufficient reviews, delivery
- **Action:** Premium content - style guides, material details, expert badges
- **Conversion Drivers:** Detailed specifications, reliability, expert validation

#### 💰 Price-Sensitive (1.4%, 5 users)
- **Wishlist Use:** Price monitoring tool - waiting for discounts
- **Friction:** Price barriers, waiting for sales
- **Action:** Price-drop alerts, personalized discounts, waitlist
- **Conversion Drivers:** Discounts, sales alerts, dynamic pricing

#### 🔄 Repeat Buyers (4.1%, 15 users)
- **Wishlist Use:** Quick decisions, high engagement - **CHURN RISK**
- **Friction:** Delivery reliability (blocking re-orders)
- **Action:** Delivery SLA improvements, fast-track returns
- **Conversion Drivers:** Delivery guarantees, faster resolution, loyalty rewards

**Evidence Quote:** *"Quality-conscious: Need detailed fabric info before buying"*

---

### Q10: What unmet needs emerge consistently across user conversations?
**Priority Unmet Needs (Ranked by Impact & Consistency):**

#### 1. **Delivery Reliability System** (8.8% mentions)
- *What users need:* Guaranteed on-time delivery, real-time tracking, fast resolution
- *What's missing:* Delivery guarantees, proactive notifications, compensation for delays
- **Build:** Real-time tracking, delivery SLA guarantees, simplified returns

#### 2. **Fit & Sizing Intelligence** (1.4% + high implicit impact)
- *What users need:* Confidence in apparel fit before purchase
- *What's missing:* Virtual try-on, size recommendations, fit guides
- **Build:** Virtual try-on AR, AI fit prediction, size comparison tools

#### 3. **Richer Product Information** (4.7% mentions)
- *What users need:* Styling guidance, material details, care instructions
- *What's missing:* Style guides, outfit combinations, care videos
- **Build:** AI-generated styling guides, care instruction videos, material specs

#### 4. **Price Intelligence Features** (3.0% mentions)
- *What users need:* Price-drop alerts, personalized discounts
- *What's missing:* Wishlist price monitoring, dynamic offers
- **Build:** Price-drop alerts, waitlist feature, personalized discounts

#### 5. **Social Proof & Reviews** (1.9% mentions)
- *What users need:* Community validation, expert recommendations
- *What's missing:* More reviews, expert badges, verified-buyer indicators
- **Build:** Expert badges, review incentives, verified-buyer indicators

#### 6. **Smart Comparison Tools** (1.7% mentions)
- *What users need:* Easy side-by-side comparison of similar items
- *What's missing:* In-app comparison feature
- **Build:** Comparison tool, side-by-side specs, quick switch

**Evidence Quote:** *"Need virtual try-on or better size guide before purchasing"*

## 🔍 Data Quality Note

**Important Context:** The review dataset contains **limited explicit wishlist mentions** (only 0.8% directly mention "wishlist"). However:

1. **Friction signals are strong** - Delivery issues, missing info, pricing concerns all clearly block purchase
2. **Behaviors are inferred** from patterns - "Waiting for sale," "need more info," "hesitation" = typical wishlist behavior
3. **Segment data is robust** - New customers (77.7%), quality-conscious (16.8%), etc. show clear patterns

**For deeper insights**, future analysis should:
- Collect wishlist-specific event data (add to wishlist, view wishlist, remove, convert)
- Conduct user interviews on wishlist decision-making
- Analyze wishlist-to-purchase time lag and conversion rates by segment
- Track price monitoring and alert engagement
