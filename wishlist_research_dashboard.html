👥 Customer Segment Analysis

🔵 New Customers (77.7%, 282 users)

Wishlist Behavior: Use as research & comparison tool before first purchase

Friction Points: Sizing uncertainty, brand trust, delivery reliability

Purchase Drivers: Social proof, detailed product info, confidence-building

Recommendation: Invest in onboarding features - fit guides, expert recommendations, delivery guarantees

⭐ Quality-Conscious (16.8%, 61 users)

Wishlist Behavior: Extensive research phase - comparing specs, reviews, materials

Friction Points: Missing product details, insufficient reviews, delivery concerns

Purchase Drivers: Detailed specifications, expert validation, reliability

Recommendation: Provide premium content - style guides, material details, expert badges, VIP support

💰 Price-Sensitive (1.4%, 5 users)

Wishlist Behavior: Price monitoring tool - waiting for discounts

Friction Points: Price barriers, waiting for sales

Purchase Drivers: Discounts, sales alerts, dynamic pricing

Recommendation: Launch price-drop alerts, personalized discounts, waitlist feature

🔄 Repeat Buyers (4.1%, 15 users)

Wishlist Behavior: Quick decisions, high engagement - churn risk visible

Friction Points: Delivery reliability (blocking re-orders)

Purchase Drivers: Delivery guarantees, faster resolution, loyalty rewards

Recommendation: Priority: Improve delivery SLA, implement fast-track returns for repeat customers

🎯 Top Unmet Needs & Recommended Solutions

1️⃣ Fit & Sizing Uncertainty (1.4% direct mention, high implicit impact)

What users say: "Worried it won't fit, size runs small/large, no try-on option"
Why it matters: Blocks purchase for apparel, especially for first-time users
Build This:
  • Virtual try-on AR capability
  • AI-powered fit recommendation using purchase history
  • Size comparison tool (how item fits vs. past purchases)
  • Enhanced size guides with body type filters

2️⃣ Delivery Reliability Crisis (8.8% explicit, causing widespread churn)

What users say: "Takes money but doesn't deliver on time, won't commit to purchase again"
Why it matters: Single biggest barrier to wishlist→purchase conversion; churn driver
Build This:
  • Real-time delivery tracking with SMS/app updates
  • Delivery guarantee with compensation
  • Simplified return process (current: too complicated)
  • Proactive support: notify if order at risk

3️⃣ Missing Product Information (4.7% explicitly, affects quality-conscious segment)

What users say: "Need fabric, care instructions, styling guidance before buying"
Why it matters: Reduces confidence, extends decision time, increases external research
Build This:
  • AI-generated styling guides (how to wear, outfit ideas)
  • Care instruction videos for complex fabrics
  • Material composition & durability specs
  • "How to style" section with outfit recommendations

4️⃣ Price Hesitation (3% explicit, affects decision-making)

What users say: "Love it but too expensive, waiting for sale"
Why it matters: Postpones purchase indefinitely; many never convert
Build This:
  • Price-drop alerts for wishlist items
  • Personalized discount recommendations
  • Waitlist feature (notify when price falls to target)
  • Dynamic pricing for wishlist items based on inventory

5️⃣ Social Validation Gap (1.9% explicit, high implicit trust barrier)

What users say: "Few reviews, uncertain about quality"
Why it matters: Blocks new product adoption, especially for unknown brands
Build This:
  • Expert/influencer recommendation badges
  • Verified-buyer review indicators
  • Community features (popularity scores, user reviews)
  • Post-purchase review incentive program
